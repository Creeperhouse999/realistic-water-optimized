# Realistic Water - OPTIMIZED 1.3-perf.2

**Modified, unofficial fork of [Realistic Water](https://github.com/sskaffy/Realistic-Water) by Skaffy.** For Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Code written with AI (Claude), reviewed and tested by the maintainer.

## Highlights
- **Tabbed control panel** (`K`, `/waterpanel`, `/water gui`): presets, quick settings, Auto FPS, world rules and every mod setting.
- **Presets**: Lowest, Ocean, Tsunami (default), Balanced, Quality.
- **Auto FPS** keeps your target frame rate by changing view distance and water detail. It never goes above your own water settings, your settings come back when it's off (also after a crash), and new limits: Min view, Above my view.
- **Realistic world** game rules: lakes, ponds, waterfalls and lava pools near you turn realistic, one whole body at a time. Oceans stay vanilla. Waterfall tops become springs.
- **Ocean sink**: realistic water flowing into vanilla water is absorbed instead of spreading the simulation across the sea.
- Big floods no longer switch the simulation off (GPU memory guard, scaled to your video memory).

## Download
`realistic-water-optimized-1.3-perf.2.jar`: replace the original Realistic Water jar with it; don't install both. Needs Fabric API. Starts on the Tsunami preset; the others are one click away in the panel.

## Tested in game
On an MSI Katana 15 (i7-13620H, RTX 4070 Laptop 8 GB, 16 GB RAM) at 4K: control panel and tabs, presets, Auto FPS up/down and restore, conversion of a 75-block and a 2,000-block lake with no hitch, waterfall spring, lava basin, ocean staying vanilla, big floods with Auto FPS on.

Still to check: crash recovery of Auto FPS settings, falling-sand conversion, the memory guard on 4-6 GB cards. See the issues labelled `untested`.
