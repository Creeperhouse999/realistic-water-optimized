# Realistic Water - OPTIMIZED 1.3-perf.3

**Modified, unofficial fork of [Realistic Water](https://github.com/sskaffy/Realistic-Water) by Skaffy.** For Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Code written with AI (Claude), reviewed and tested by the maintainer.

## Fixed
- **Full tanks no longer burst through their walls when Auto FPS changes quality.** Changing water detail rebuilds all water from blocks, and in testing a full, calm 13-block-deep glass tank leaked straight through intact glass after the rebuild. Auto FPS now only changes water detail when no realistic water is simulated at all, and never forces it. It still adjusts view distance, physics steps, surface detail and foam.

## Changed
- Shorter help texts in the control panel.

## Download
`realistic-water-optimized-1.3-perf.3.jar`: replace the original Realistic Water jar (and older versions of this fork) with it. Needs Fabric API.

Tested on an MSI Katana 15 (i7-13620H, RTX 4070 Laptop 8 GB, 16 GB RAM) at 4K.
